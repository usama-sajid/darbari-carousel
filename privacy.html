Darbari

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Darbari is an offline music and video player. Your privacy is important to us, and Darbari is designed to keep your personal media on your device.

Information we collect

Darbari does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, user accounts, or tracking technologies.

Media access

Darbari requests access to audio and video files so it can find, organize, and play media stored on your device. This media is processed locally and is not uploaded to us or shared with third parties.

Internet access and featured content

The app may use an internet connection to retrieve a public configuration file and featured images for its home-screen carousel. Tapping a featured item may open an external website in your browser. Darbari does not send your local media or personal information with these requests.

Background playback

Darbari uses a foreground media-playback service so audio can continue playing while the app is in the background and playback controls can appear in the system notification area.

Children's privacy

Darbari is intended for users aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Darbari's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will always appear on this page.
